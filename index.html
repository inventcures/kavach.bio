Epidemic preparedness for India

Kavach

कवच · "shield"

An effort to help India prepare for the next epidemic. The chance of one in any given year is small. If it comes, we want health workers to have respirators that fit, stock that has not expired, and a country that is ready to act.

Why respirators, and why now

3 masks

During the 2018 Nipah outbreak in Kozhikode, field staff were at first told to wear three ordinary masks at once, because few N95s were available.

Deccan Chronicle, May 2018
None

India has no national respirator stockpile, and none has been formally proposed. Calls for one have come only from think tanks.

Carnegie India 2020; ORF 2023
≈3 crore

Health and frontline workers India counted in its COVID-19 vaccine priority list: the people a reserve should protect first.

BOOM, Dec 2020

The idea in four steps

  1. Protect workers firstAbout 1 crore health workers, then about 2 crore frontline workers.
  2. Masks that fitTest which respirators fit Indian faces before buying at scale.
  3. Stock that stays freshRotate it through routine hospital use, with one owner and its own budget.
  4. Start smallA 12-month pilot with about 5,000 health workers in Kerala.
