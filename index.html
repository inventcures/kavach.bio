Kavach

Kavach means "shield" in Hindi. This is a small effort to help prepare India for the next epidemic, so that in the unlikely event one comes, the country is ready and alert. We are starting with respiratory protection for health workers.

  • A respiratory protection stockpile for Indiadraft

    White paper: why India needs a respirator reserve, what other countries learned, and a pilot design for Kerala.

  • India stockpile plannerprototype

    Pick a state and an outbreak scenario to see how many respirators are needed and what they cost.

  • India Respirator Fit Panel: study protocoldraft

    A proposed study of Indian facial dimensions and respirator fit among health workers.

  • Mask drillprototype

    A phone game for practising how to choose, fit, check and remove a mask.

These are early drafts, built with AI assistance and open about it. Sources are cited in each document. Comments and corrections are welcome.
